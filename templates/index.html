04 / THE HUMAN SIDE

It started with curiosity.

Long before I knew what a server was, I wanted to know how the things I loved actually worked.

My introduction to technology came through video games: first on a console, then on a computer. Modding games led me into configuration files, code, and the logic behind software. Eventually, learning how systems work became more exciting than simply using them.

That curiosity grew into programming, databases, Linux, networking, servers and cloud infrastructure. During technical education and two internships, I had the opportunity to work with real IT environments — from cables and access points to Linux hosting and enterprise application deployment.

Today I build projects that connect these disciplines. I'm especially interested in cloud engineering, DevOps, systems architecture and security — and in becoming the kind of engineer who understands the full path from idea to production.
